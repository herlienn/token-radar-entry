Every term tested, twice

Kept only if it beats size-only on BOTH leave-one-out and walk-forward by ≥1% and wins over half the 200 random splits. Nothing did — so the value model stays size-only. The control (another row's growth) "wins" LOO by 4.6% and still fails: at n=90 the median miss is noisy, which is why the two-test rule exists.

Do the ranges tell the truth?

Ranges are prediction intervals from the leave-one-out residuals — not the bootstrap parameter intervals, which print far too narrow (only 6.7% of real caps inside P25–P75). Walk-forward P10–P90 prints a little narrow (69.6% of the stated 80%): the ranges are honest about that too.

Model & tests

Value model — size-only OLS

Odds model — 24-month horizon

Launch odds by age

Odds(A) = [F(730) − F(A)] / [1 − F(A)]: the chance a protocol still tokenless at age A records one within its first 24 months. The window closes — a protocol past 730 days without a token has ~0 chance of launching within its first 24 months, so its odds are 0.001, not a made-up number.

Cohorts: the era effect

The age curve mixes two things: older protocols have had more time to launch, AND the 2021–23 cohorts listed in a friendlier era than the 2025–26 cohorts. The table shows the era effect next to the age curve so the confound is visible, not hidden. Young protocols' "no token" labels are censored — that is why they never enter the odds fit.
